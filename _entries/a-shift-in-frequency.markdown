As summer arrives, things are changing here at the Lab. Much like particles in quantum physics, which can exist in multiple states until they're observed, we've spent the first half of the year exploring new possibilities, experimenting behind the scenes, and letting the next phase of ATI take shape.

Now it's time to collapse the wave function and share what we've been working on.

One of our biggest updates is welcoming our new Social Media + Marketing Intern, Rheala Dracer (she/her), who will be helping us amplify ATI's story, connect with our community, and share more of what happens inside the Lab.
![web-Rhaela.jpg](/uploads/web-Rhaela.jpg)

You'll also notice a few shifts in our orbit. We're retiring some of our older jewelry and design lines, participating in fewer markets, focusing on more exhibitions, and spending more time sharing our process and discoveries. Staying closer to the Lab means more time for research, experimentation, and making the kind of work that fuels ATI.

Thank you, as always, for being part of this journey. Whether you've been with us from the beginning or just recently tuned into our frequency, we're excited to have you along for this next chapter.

Now, if you'll excuse us, there are important discoveries waiting to be made.
