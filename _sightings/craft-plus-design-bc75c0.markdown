The Visual Arts Center of Richmond’s Craft + Design is a contemporary craft show that has garnered a reputation for showcasing the finest in museum-quality craft. The show is held in the historic Main Street Station’s train shed. Since the inaugural show in 1964, Craft + Design has become more than just a craft show. Today, it’s a weekend-long event featuring live artist demonstrations, family-friendly workshops, and local food and drink. You can find Rachel with her ATI project at booth 212a!
